Request Data Deletion

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This page explains how to ask for your data to be deleted from Pocket Oracle: Daily Wisdom ("PocketOracle"), the app published by Oaie Cornel-Sebastian (Cornel Oaie) on Google Play and the App Store. The App has no accounts, so the data our service providers hold is linked only to random identifiers, not to your name or email address. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How to request deletion

  1. Email corneloaie@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my PocketOracle data".
  2. If you bought Pro, include the order number from your Google Play receipt (it starts with "GPA.") or your App Store receipt, so that we can find your purchase record.
  3. We will confirm by email once your request has been handled, within one month.

What we delete

  • Your record at RevenueCat, the service that manages Pro status: your purchases and the rewarded-ad events linked to them.
  • Any Google Analytics for Firebase or Firebase Crashlytics data we are able to link to you.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Analytics and crash data that we cannot link to you is deleted automatically: Google Analytics data after up to 14 months, and Crashlytics crash reports after 90 days.
  • Without an order number we cannot find your RevenueCat record, which is linked only to a random identifier; its ad events are then kept as described in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play and the App Store keep their own records of your purchases under their own policies; we cannot delete those.
  • Advertising data is held by Google. You can change your choices with Privacy choices on the App's home screen, where available, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, or contact Google.

Data on your device

Your Journal, settings, and other App data are stored only on your device. You can delete Journal entries in the App, or delete the App to remove all of its data from your device.
